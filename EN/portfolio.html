CIVIS Days

Over the years, I have worked on the design for several editions of the same event, the ‘CIVIS Days’. Every year, this event brings together numerous stakeholders in higher education to discuss the future of international collaboration. The event takes place at a different European university each year. My role involves creating a visual design representing the city hosting the event, along with its various applications: posters, roll-ups, postcards, promotional items, social media visuals, etc. I then travel to the venue to conduct interviews and take photographs of the event.

EAIE Conference - Booth, posters, and games

EAIE is a non-profit organisation supporting those involved in the internationalisation of their institutions. It organises an annual conference.

As part of my work with CIVIS, I designed two posters that were presented at these conferences. I was also responsible for designing the CIVIS stand, as well as the promotional items and other games.

At the 2024 event, my design won the prize for best poster , and in 2025, my work took third place on the podium.

2024 event

We wanted the booth to represent a strain tation stop, the ‘CIVIS Station’. The aim was to showcase the first stage of a journey, which is depicted in the poster.

2025 event

At the 2025 event, the idea was ambitious: to create a giant ‘Where's Waldo?’ featuring the various institutions and figures from the CIVIS alliance. Several other games were also on offer: Participants had to guess in which country the university buildings depicted on maps were located, or find, in the “Where’s Waldo?”, characters representing the educational programmes offered by CIVIS.

A history of the space race (or thereabouts)

This website is my final-year project. I decided to focus on the space race, a subject that fascinates me greatly. I handled everything myself: the illustrations, design, coding and writing the text. My aim was to do everything in a way that was as much fun as possible! There are various animations, and the site is designed to be playful and interactive, with hidden elements scattered throughout.

Tools used: HTML5, CSS, JavaScript, Photoshop, Illustrator.

Illustration and painting

I’ve always loved drawing and painting. I can use illustration in many graphic design projects, but unfortunately, painting is more difficult to incorporate into projects that require changes. So I create my own projects! Since 2025, I’ve taken my watercolours with me on every trip outside Belgium. My goal: to produce at least one watercolour per trip.
